Put 5-10 chest X-ray images (.png / .jpg) in this folder. They show up in the
"Results arrived" dropdown of the demo UI.

Public TB chest X-ray datasets you can take a few images from:
- Shenzhen and Montgomery County chest X-ray sets (U.S. National Library of Medicine)
- TBX11K
- "Tuberculosis (TB) Chest X-ray Database" on Kaggle (has both Normal and TB folders)

Pick a mix of TB and normal images, and name them clearly, e.g.
tb_01.png, tb_02.png, normal_01.png. Check each dataset's licence before using it in
slides or a report.
