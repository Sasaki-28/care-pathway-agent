One patient, four people,
one AI coordinator keeping them in step.

The coordinator runs each patient's care pathway for days or months. It sleeps while it waits for the lab, the doctor or the patient, and wakes when they act. Nothing reaches the patient until a doctor signs. Choose who you are:

Clinic desk

Admit patients, see every case on one board, open a case file, and talk to the coordinator.

Lab

Doctor

Patient

Patients get an email with a private link to their own page: their messages, a plain-language status, and weekly check-ins. Open a case on the clinic desk to see its patient page.

Find a patient's page →
  1. Clinic admits the patient; AI suggests a pathway, staff confirm.
  2. Coordinator orders the tests, emails the patient, then sleeps.
  3. Lab sends results; lab and imaging agents read them.
  4. Doctor reviews and signs, or asks for more tests.
  5. Patient is told and emailed, checks in weekly; warning signs go straight back to the doctor.
